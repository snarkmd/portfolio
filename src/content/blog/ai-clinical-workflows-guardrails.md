# AI in Clinical Workflows: Guardrails First

AI features in healthcare should be designed around responsibility, not novelty.

## Useful places to start

- Summarizing long records.
- Drafting patient-friendly explanations.
- Finding missing documentation.
- Routing routine administrative tasks.

These are support tasks, not authority transfers.

## Dangerous shortcuts

The riskiest pattern is an answer that looks confident while hiding uncertainty. Clinical users need provenance, limits, and a way to inspect the source.

## A safer workflow

1. AI drafts.
2. Clinician reviews.
3. System records the final human action.
4. Corrections remain traceable.

The point is not to slow everything down. The point is to keep accountability visible.
